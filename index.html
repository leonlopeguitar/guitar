Common questions

Things people ask before booking.

Am I too old to start learning guitar?
No. Adults learn differently than children — not slower. You bring patience, focus, and the ability to connect music emotionally to your experience. I've taught students in their 30s, 40s, 50s, and 60s who went from zero to playing songs they love within months. Age is not the barrier. The right method is.
How much time do I need to practice each week?
15 minutes a day is enough to make real progress — as long as those 15 minutes are targeted. That's exactly what the written lesson summary is for: you always know what to work on, so there's no wasted time. Consistency matters far more than long sessions.
I tried before and gave up. What makes this different?
Most people quit because they were learning without a clear structure — random YouTube videos, apps that feel like games, or teachers who gave exercises disconnected from music they care about. I start by understanding exactly where you are and what you want to play, then build a path from there. You'll feel progress from the first lesson.
Can online lessons really work as well as in-person?
That was my biggest doubt too — before I started teaching this way. The honest answer: for most people, online lessons work just as well, and in some ways better. You learn in your own space, with your own guitar, in a comfortable environment with no travel time lost. Screen sharing lets me see your fretting hand up close and annotate TABs in real time. Every student I've taught online has progressed. The key is a stable internet connection and a decent webcam angle — I'll help you set it up in the first session.
What style of music do you teach?
Whatever you want to play. Rock, pop, blues, folk, fingerpicking, Latin — I adapt the curriculum to your taste. Students have played everything from Foo Fighters to Blackbird to Autumn Leaves. You bring the songs. I'll make them work for your level.
How does the free trial lesson work?
You book a 30-minute slot, we meet on Google Meet, and I do a quick placement to understand your current level and goals. You'll leave with a real song to work on and a clear sense of where lessons would take you. There's no pressure and no obligation to continue — but most students book their first paid cycle on the same call.
How are lessons structured and how much do they cost?
Lessons are 1-on-1 on Google Meet, 60 minutes each. I teach in 5-week cycles so you have a consistent rhythm and enough time to see meaningful progress between check-ins. After each lesson you get a written summary with your practice plan for the week. Pricing is shared after the trial — it's significantly less than local in-person lessons.
Do I need to read sheet music?
No. We work with guitar TAB, which is much quicker to learn and more than enough to play any song you want. If you're interested in reading standard notation later, we can cover it — but it's never a requirement.
