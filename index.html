The curriculum

9 levels. One clear path.

Every level has mapped outcomes, real songs, and specific techniques. You advance when you're ready — not on a fixed schedule.

Level 1
Foundations
Guitar anatomy, tuning, TAB reading, pick technique, alternate picking.
Recommended songs
Seven Nation Army, Smoke on the Water
Level 2
Open Chords & Strumming
C, G, D, Em, Am — steady rhythm, chord switching, capo use.
Recommended songs
Knockin' on Heaven's Door, Get Lucky, We Will Rock You
Level 3
Power Chords & Rock Riffs
Movable shapes, palm muting, 12-bar blues, slides, string bending basics.
Recommended songs
Satisfaction, Smells Like Teen Spirit, Sunshine of Your Love
Level 4
Scales & Melody
Pentatonic scale (3 positions), vibrato, hammer-ons, improvisation over backing tracks.
Recommended songs
Wish You Were Here, Stand by Me
Level 5
Chords & Triads
Major/minor construction, CAGED intro, I–V–vi–IV progressions, arpeggiation.
Recommended songs
Let It Be, Happier, No Woman No Cry
Level 6
Fingerpicking
PIMA patterns, Travis picking, natural harmonics, combining picking and strumming.
Recommended songs
Blackbird, Dust in the Wind, Fast Car
Level 7
Barre Chords & CAGED
E and A-shape barres, full CAGED system, key changes, string muting.
Recommended songs
Wonderwall, Hotel California (rhythm)
Level 8
Lead Guitar
All 5 pentatonic positions, major/minor scales, bends, modes intro, call & response phrasing.
Recommended songs
Stairway to Heaven (solo), Pride and Joy
Level 9
Rhythm & Genre Expansion
7th chords, extended chords, funk 16ths, odd time signatures, hybrid picking.
Recommended songs
Little Wing, Gravity, Autumn Leaves
🏆 Levels 10–12: Mastery Track — advanced scales, modes, sweep arpeggios, and full stylistic fluency. Personalised to your goals.

The arrangement difference

Your song, your level.

Most teachers hand you a full song and tell you to learn it. If it's too hard, you're on your own.

I do it differently. Every song is arranged specifically for where you are right now — the right chord voicings, the right picking pattern, the right difficulty. Not a stripped-down version. A real arrangement built for you.

As you progress, the arrangement evolves with you. The same song can grow from Level 2 all the way to Level 7 — each version more complete, more expressive, more yours.

Level 2 arrangement
Wonderwall — Oasis
Open chords, basic strumming pattern, full song structure
Level 5 arrangement
Wonderwall — Oasis
Triads, muted strums, richer chord voicings, capo use
Level 7 arrangement
Wonderwall — Oasis
Barre chords, full CAGED positions, expressive dynamics
